## Implementation Notes

- **Hosting & Serving**: All files are placed locally inside `frontend/assets/products/`. They are statically served by Express (`express.static`) at `/assets/products/...` with zero external dependencies.
- **Fallbacks**: If a custom user-created product lacks an image or encounters a network loading failure, the client generates a dynamic SVG placeholder embedded with the product's actual Brand, Title, and Category.
- **Pricing & Discounts**: MRPs and Selling Prices are mathematically verified (`discount_percent = Math.round(((mrp - price) / mrp) * 100)`).
- **Reviews**: Customer reviews displayed on the demonstration website feature verified purchase indicators and are clearly denoted as sample reviews for academic demonstration purposes.
